Heat pump electricity — why it's free

The heat pump system consumes ~85,000 kWh/year. The farm already exports 428,210 kWh of surplus PV to the grid. Redirecting a fraction of this surplus through a Renewable Energy Community (CER) scheme covers the heat pump's electricity at zero marginal cost — turning an energy expense into a net income opportunity.
